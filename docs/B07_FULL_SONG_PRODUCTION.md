## Definition of done

1. The final export contains the whole song, with its ending preserved and no
   unintentional gaps, repeated filler or missing scene coverage. Frame/sample
   quantization is measured and distinguished from lost audio.
2. All ten recognizable settings appear, with exactly five day and five night
   concert environments, consistent performer appearance and microphone.
3. Visible lead singing follows the actual vocal phrases throughout the edit.
   Featured crowd singing is controlled; mass crowd motion conveys participation
   without claiming word-perfect synchronization of every distant face.
4. The edit delivers the planned story, varied angles, close-ups, aerials,
   energetic cuts and validated orbit coverage. Shots rejected for identity,
   mouth, hand, microphone or scene defects remain open until corrected or
   replaced with equivalent coverage that still meets the creative requirement.
5. Successful takes, source audio, revisions and exports survive worker/GPU
   removal. Selective replacement preserves earlier successful takes and cuts.
6. Actual measurements and any unresolved bills are recorded honestly. GPU
   teardown is confirmed separately from render success.
7. John reviews the complete real video. Fictional automated checks do not stand
   in for this creative acceptance.

Completion proves this measured production path and configuration. It does not
establish quality or cost for every musical style, language or duration.

## Production and correction loop

Prepare the lyric/beat map, exact audio windows, references, shot specifications
and model configurations before the main rental. Queue compatible work so that
one shot follows another; keep initialized models resident when possible.
Render order may differ from editing order. Every shot retains its timeline
position and exact vocal interval.

After each output, validate and checkpoint it outside the GPU. Retain usable
results. Record the failure category for rejected work, diagnose the cause,
change the relevant prompt, reference, timing, settings or code, and retry only
the affected shot or operation. Repeated identical failure requires a changed
approach, rather than blind replay. No separate owner permission is needed for
each ordinary corrective retry within the eventual approved spending scope.

Build an evolving full-length cut as coverage arrives. It may be labeled
incomplete while gaps remain, but must never be reported as the finished film.
Revalidate changed segments and final assembly instead of rerunning successful
unrelated work. Pause paid resources if there is no executable work while a
code repair, unavailable input or owner decision is pending; resume from saved
state when ready.

Each defect record needs: project and shot IDs, attempt ID, symptoms and evidence,
suspected/confirmed cause, exact correction or commit, next attempt result,
retained take and timeline revision, elapsed time, estimated/posted cost and
closure status. Each retry creates a new attempt; accepted history is immutable.

Never retry an ambiguous Pod-create response without reconciling the original
request. Retrying media does not justify duplicate infrastructure. Current
historical reservations, cleanup tracking and seven consumed grants remain intact.
